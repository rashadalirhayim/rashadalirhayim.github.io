I'm so glad, I finally submitted my first paper for review! It has been almost two years since I wrote my thesis and it feels great to finally have it out there. I am so nervous about the feedback, but hope that my hard work will pay off and I will publish my thesis soon.

So exactly how do you publish your first paper?

The process of publishing is different for every field, but there are some constants. Your first priority should be publishing your thesis as a paper. My thesis took almost a year to put together so it made sense for me to use this as my first article submission. It took some time to edit before I could submit it, but overall it wasn't too difficult.

## Where should I publish my thesis?

The best way to get recognition is to publish in a peer-reviewed recognized journal in your field. Journals are either open-access or traditional publishing. Open-access journals are published digitally and provide full access to the content, but there's an important distinction in their format. Traditional publishing journals typically have higher standards for formatting and submission requirements than open-access journals. If you want your work to be professionally edited before it's submitted, this might be the route for you.

Once you have decided between OA or traditional modes of publishing, find out which journals accept papers that are similar to your subject field. Research papers, search for keywords and read the abstracts. You may also want to ask your advisor or professor what journals are the most recognized in your field.

It's important to remember that top journals are typically more strict with their formatting guidelines and deadlines for submissions. They also have higher standards for quality content, so take some time before submitting your paper!

## Read the Guidelines

Journals provide instructions on how to submit your paper and what their review process is. It's important to read these guidelines before you start submitting because they may have different formatting requirements than other journals, or charge a fee for submission.

## Edit and Format your Thesis

If this is the first time you are publishing a paper in any field it can be overwhelming but there are some simple steps that should help make the experience enjoyable! Formatting an article might seem like a daunting task, but thankfully there are many resources available online from journal websites and blog posts that will walk you through each step of the process.

The most important thing when preparing your manuscript is ensuring that you have met all formatting standards for your chosen journal or publisher. You want to follow their guidelines exactly so they understand how serious you are about getting this work out there!

See what they require from authors, such as formatting requirements, word counts or deadlines. Reviewing these points before submitting help reduce chances of mistakes due to last minute changes or edits during the review process.

## Proofread Before Submitting

Take some time before submitting to review your work for any grammatical or typographical errors or missing words. Your piece should be well-written and free from typos so that it is taken seriously by reviewers who may not have access to the original source material you are referencing in order to verify accuracy of information presented. The more careless mistakes you make, the less likely you will receive constructive feedback on how to improve writing skills and content quality as these flaws detract from scholarly research process.

You can also ask the editor how long they typically take to review submissions and what their response time is. The earlier in advance of submission you get started with any revisions or edits, the better off you'll be when it comes down to submitting!

## Final Thoughts

- It takes a lot of time and effort to publish a thesis; it can be both intimidating because of all the steps involved and exciting.
- Start as early as possible and look for opportunities as they arise.
